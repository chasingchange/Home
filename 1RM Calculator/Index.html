How this works & research sources

Estimated max. Reps-to-failure = reps + (10 − RPE), using the repetitions-in-reserve RPE scale (Zourdos et al., 2016; Helms et al., 2016). We average four published prediction equations (Epley, Brzycki, Mayhew, Wathen). A true single at RPE 10 is used as-is.

Accuracy. Predictions are most accurate from lower-rep sets, so a heavy 1–5 rep set is best (Reynolds et al., 2006). Estimates past ~10 reps-to-failure are unreliable. Most equations predict squat and bench well but underestimate deadlift maxes, so your real deadlift may be higher (LeSuer et al., 1997).

Scores. IPF GL = total × 100 ÷ (A − B·e−C·BW), with IPF-published coefficients by sex and division (IPF, 2020). DOTS and Wilks use their published 4th/5th-order polynomial coefficients. Bodyweight is in kg.
