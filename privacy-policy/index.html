Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Chasing Change ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect across chasingchange.com and its tools, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information — if you sign up for the Client Portal, we collect your email address and the profile and coaching data (workouts, check-ins, progress notes) you or your coach add, stored securely via our database provider, Supabase.
  • Coaching applications — when you apply for 1:1 coaching or contact us, the information you submit (name, email, and your answers) is collected through our form provider, Tally.
  • Newsletter subscriptions — if you subscribe to Science of Chasing Change, your email address is collected and managed by our newsletter provider, Beehiiv.
  • Tool inputs — calculators and worksheets on this site (macros, 1RM, career fit, and similar tools) may process the numbers or answers you enter locally in your browser to show you a result. We don't require an account to use them.
  • Usage information — standard technical data your browser sends (such as pages visited and device/browser type) may be logged by our hosting provider for security and performance purposes.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your Client Portal account and deliver coaching services
  • To review and respond to coaching applications and contact requests
  • To send newsletter content to subscribers, and coaching-related updates to clients
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the site and its tools

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing and third parties

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the site, each acting under their own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting for the Client Portal
  • Tally — form handling for coaching applications and contact requests
  • Beehiiv — newsletter delivery and subscriber management

We do not otherwise share your information with third parties except where required by law, to protect our rights, or with your consent.

Your choices

  • You can access, update, or request deletion of your Client Portal account and data at any time by contacting us.
  • You can unsubscribe from the newsletter using the link in any email.
  • Most calculators and worksheets on this site don't store your inputs on our servers, so there's usually nothing to request beyond what's in your own browser.

Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
